    Good to know

    Snow, driving and packing

    🧥

    Pack for the cold

    • Furano and Biei: about −2°C by day, down to −9°C at night
    • Thermal layers, a waterproof jacket, gloves and a beanie
    • Shoes with good grip, or slip-on cleats for icy paths
    🚐

    Driving

    • Not booked yet: 4WD minivan for 5 with winter tires and an ETC card, Nov 25–30, picked up and returned at New Chitose. Mary Rose and Kenn will sort it out on Nov 23.
    • Bring each driver's international driving permit and passport
    • Watch for black ice in the mornings. Aim to arrive before dark.
    🎿

    Snow and closures

    • Furano Ski Resort opens Nov 28 with only a few runs. Tubing parks mostly open in late December, so bring your own cheap sleds.
    • The Asahidake ropeway is closed Nov 16 – Dec 10
    • Check the light-up dates for Ningle Terrace and the Blue Pond before you go
